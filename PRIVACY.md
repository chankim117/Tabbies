# Tabbies privacy policy

_Last updated: October 19, 2026_

Tabbies is a tab manager for Chrome. It shows your windows, tab groups and tabs as a tree, lets you move and close them, and lets you "nap" tabs (close them and keep their links to open later).

**In short: everything Tabbies sees stays on your computer. Tabbies has no server, no account, no analytics and no ads, and it never sends, sells or shares your data.**

## What Tabbies reads, and why

- **Your open tabs and tab groups.** Titles, addresses, site icons, and whether a tab is pinned, muted, playing sound or asleep. This is how Tabbies draws the tree and lets you search, move, sort and close tabs. Chrome describes this permission as "Read your browsing history".
- **Page previews (optional, off by default).** Only if you turn on Settings → Page previews, Tabbies takes a small picture of the tab you are looking at, to show when you point at that tab later. Chrome asks for your permission first ("Read and change all your data on all websites"). Tabbies uses this permission only to take these pictures. It never reads, changes or collects anything inside the pages. Turning previews off deletes every picture and gives the permission back.
- **Bookmarks (optional, off by default).** Only if you turn on Settings → Sync naps, Tabbies keeps your naps in a bookmarks folder named "Tabbies Naps". Chrome asks for your permission first. Tabbies only reads and changes that one folder.

Tabbies does not read your browsing history beyond the tabs that are open, and it does not read what you type or see on web pages.

## What Tabbies stores, and where

All of it is stored by Chrome on your computer:

- **Naps** (the links, titles and site icon addresses of napped tabs) and the names you give your windows: in Chrome's local extension storage.
- **Page preview pictures:** in memory only. They are forgotten when Chrome closes, and at most the 50 most recent are kept.
- **Settings:** in Chrome's sync storage.

## Chrome Sync

If you're signed in to Chrome with sync on, Chrome itself copies your Tabbies **settings** to your other computers. If you turn on **Sync naps**, Chrome Sync also copies the "Tabbies Naps" bookmarks folder, like any other bookmarks. This is Chrome's own sync, run by Google under Google's privacy policy. Tabbies has no access to it beyond your own Chrome profile.

## Network requests

Tabbies doesn't contact any server of its own. To show site icons, it uses the icon address Chrome reports for each tab. Chrome usually loads these from its cache; otherwise the icon is fetched from the website it belongs to, as any web page would.

## Removing your data

- Delete individual naps, or all of them (Naps ⋯ → Delete all naps…), at any time.
- Turning off Page previews deletes all preview pictures.
- Uninstalling Tabbies deletes everything it stored on your computer. The "Tabbies Naps" bookmarks folder, if you used Sync naps, stays in your bookmarks until you delete it.
